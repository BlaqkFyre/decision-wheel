# Decision Wheel

A single-file app (`index.html`) that shows evangelical and end-times beliefs as a radial wheel, and compares them with theologians, authors and survey users.

- Open `index.html` in a browser to use it. Once it's hosted (GitHub Pages or similar) it also works offline on phone and PC, and can be added to the home screen.
- **Visitors** can view everything and add their own answers (kept in their browser), then send them to Luke.
- **Admin** (Luke) unlocks editing, importing and deleting on a device with a passphrase. In Chrome or Edge on the PC, Survey → **Link this file** saves changes straight back into `index.html`.
